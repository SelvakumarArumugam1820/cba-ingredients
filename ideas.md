## Selected Approach: Provenance Ledger

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial modernism with references to Swiss information design, premium food journals, and the quiet precision of a laboratory notebook.

### Core Principles
1. Lead with clarity: every section answers what CBA supplies, who it serves, or how the relationship works.
2. Use asymmetry as confidence: offset columns, left-anchored headlines, and deliberate white space replace generic centered hero layouts.
3. Balance science and appetite: precise type and linework sit beside tactile ingredient photography.
4. Make the Saudi base visible without becoming decorative: local address, regional service language, and global sourcing cues work together.

### Color Philosophy
The base is a deep ink navy that signals reliability and creates a strong frame for warm food imagery. Sand and milk-white provide the calm of a clean specification sheet. Saffron gold is reserved for moments of value, direction, and contact; it is the ownable accent that turns a functional supplier into a memorable partner. Terracotta and leaf green appear only as small category signals.

### Layout Paradigm
A split editorial canvas: a fixed-feeling left rail for identity and navigation, with content flowing through offset bands and wide product-category panels. Sections should feel composed like a trade journal spread rather than a symmetrical SaaS landing page.

### Signature Elements
1. A thin saffron route-line motif that moves from the Saudi marker through product categories.
2. Small uppercase labels paired with oversized serif editorial headlines.
3. Ingredient swatches and material notes that make each category feel tangible.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like opening a product specification or moving through a supply route: direct, tactile, and quick. Hover states reveal a little more context, buttons press visibly, and mobile navigation stays calm and legible.

### Animation
Use restrained entrance reveals with 30–70ms stagger, line-drawing route animations, gentle image scale on hover, and small translate/opacity transitions under 300ms. Avoid perpetual motion except for a subtle hero grain drift. Respect prefers-reduced-motion for all non-essential motion.

### Typography System
Display: Cormorant Garamond, 600–700, for editorial headlines and major statements. UI/body: Manrope, 400–700, for navigation, product lists, metadata, and CTAs. Headlines use tight leading, body copy uses generous leading, and labels remain uppercase with tracking for a catalog-like tone.

### Brand Essence
CBA Ingredients is the Saudi sourcing partner for food businesses that need quality raw materials, dependable availability, and straightforward support without the friction of fragmented procurement.
Personality: assured, precise, welcoming.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are concise and confident. CTAs are practical and specific. Microcopy sounds like a knowledgeable partner, never a generic marketplace.

Example lines:
- "Build the next product on better foundations."
- "Tell us what you are formulating. We will help you source it."

### Wordmark & Logo
Use the supplied gold CBA Ingredients emblem as the primary mark. Pair it with a custom-styled wordmark lockup: small uppercase CBA Ingredients beside the emblem on desktop, and a compact emblem-only mark on mobile. Never redraw the supplied symbol or place it inside a generic badge.

### Signature Brand Color
Saffron Gold — #C99A3D. It represents value, warmth, and the finishing note that makes a formulation feel complete.

## Content Guardrails

The competitor websites informed only broad category conventions such as product navigation, contact visibility, and application-led positioning. No competitor copy, logos, imagery, claims, or distinctive layouts are reproduced. The supplied CBA Ingredients company description, contact details, and Saudi National Address information are the source of truth for this website.

## Style Decisions

- Use dark ink navy, sand, milk-white, and saffron gold; do not use purple gradients or neon effects.
- Keep the hero left-aligned with a strong image field on the right.
- Use category names from the supplied product portfolio, with original descriptive copy.
- Show the Saudi National Address in a dedicated contact panel using the supplied details.

## Style Decisions

- Every major page must include a provenance-system element such as a route line, indexed marker, source/application/supply label, or material note.
- Page composition should favor offset columns, rail-like identity cues, and specification-spread structures over centered stacked sections.
- The navy serif plus saffron italic pairing remains a signature moment, but catalog/specification headings should alternate with it to keep the voice disciplined.
- Saudi provenance should be structural: use Dammam / Saudi Arabia route language and geographic cues as part of the visual system, not only as footer copy.
